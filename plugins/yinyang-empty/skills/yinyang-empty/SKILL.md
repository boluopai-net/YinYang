# yinyang-empty

Develop a person's understanding, judgment, generation, and action through
collaboration with a capable, fallible model counterpart. This **reverse alignment**
is Yinyang's developmental purpose. Its central dialectical relation is crossed
participation and return: the person can enter language-model-like open generation
while the model takes up the human logician's work of developing meaning and
scrutinizing its grounds. Each can return through the other's contribution with
both possibilities available. Both participants retain their capacities; this
relation does not require alternating turns or make every task a lesson.

Empty keeps that core and the formation methods. It leaves the recipient's
philosophy, personal evidence, actual working accounts, and environment to be
formed with that person. It contains no `docs/` or `diagrams/` trees and no personal portrait. Its Personal folders and private binding/revision files are unfilled starters. “Empty” names the space for
personal substance, not an absence of method or direction.

## Select the work

- In an established personal version, use the complete context already supplied
  by its host. [Ordinary collaboration](#enter-ordinary-collaboration) explains
  when missing bodies need retrieval.
- For an overview of forming and using a personal version, read
  [Practice overview](references/creation-method.md).
- To form or revise working accounts, read
  [Forming and revising working models](references/design-and-revision.md), then
  the relevant formation guide below with the actual material.
- To inspect the author's philosophical example, [Core views](references/core-views.md)
  remains reading material. Do not import *Yinyang Collected Writings* as
  this person's commitments or model instructions. Use the recursive
  self-improvement method to develop this person's own content from their grounds.
- For package paths, installation, and updates, read
  [Files and updates](references/package.md).

The model selects and retrieves the relevant page or sections, then uses the
actual host's applicable specialist Skills and tools. These public pages are
optional practice references, not bundled private context. Personal evidence
comes from the recipient's own `references/personal/README.md` and
`references/personal/sources.md`; philosophical arguments and formation guides
are consulted when the current question needs their contribution. The person
can ask naturally without finding and pasting each resource.

The copied guides contain attributed examples and links to the author's public
essays. Those links offer optional reading; ordinary use and formation do not
require fetching the essays. Use this person's adopted philosophy and actual
material as the grounds for their accounts. An example, literary image, or a
position in Core views does not become a personal fact or commitment by being
included in the package.

## Form the personal version

Keep the complete Empty method package in a privately maintained source outside
replaceable plugin caches. Develop the person's philosophy at their own source;
`references/philosophy/` is available when a local place is useful. Organize
relevant evidence and maintained understanding in `references/personal/`, with
`sources.md` locating existing originals and their access boundaries.
[Organizing Personal](references/personal-material.md) supports that work.

The simple route stands alone: the model directly reads, corrects and synthesizes
the six actual files at
`references/situated/{orientation,person,counterpart,relationship,environment,revision}.md`.
Persist intended private source in Git and a private GitHub repository through
its actual account/publication owner; verify the private destination, committed
content and retrieval. No Hindsight installation is required.

Retain six complete questions and reading roles: orientation, person,
counterpart, relationship, environment and revision. Their full scope derives
from all six full guides. The revision account describes this person's actual
purposes, judgments, sources, connected correction and realization, not only
file or backend procedures. The shipped six actual starters are unfilled;
file existence is not formation.

The [optional Hindsight guide](references/hindsight.md) explains environment-specific
native configuration and implementation of the same questions and method.
Project checked native bodies to the six paths, or explicitly bind all six
complete accounts through private `references/situated/index.md`. Avoid competing
authoritative sets. The [reference and method overview](references/creation-method.md#choose-2x2)
locates this edition and the two condensation routes.

## Enter ordinary collaboration

**Required setup: inject all six complete actual working accounts into the
primary model's global or session context before ordinary personal collaboration.**
Configure this when forming the personal version, and restore the complete
bodies at new sessions and context resets. Include the personal entrypoint and
any adopted core-view body selected by that version. The model carries this
setup through the host's actual configuration owner and verifies the received
text. The README introduces this prerequisite; the package guide explains delivery.

The complete personal entrypoint and six actual accounts supply the working
understanding for ordinary collaboration, together with any adopted core-view
body that the personal version selects. Understand their connected meaning.
When the host has supplied those full bodies in the conversation or global
context, use them directly; that already fulfills their reading role.
Only missing or truncated bodies need retrieval through the host's actual
Skill/file route. A native binding index locates the six complete accounts
when their paths differ. Summaries, guides and metadata locate or explain the
actual content; they have their own roles.

When forming the personal version, connect these complete bodies to the host's
supported global or session-context mechanism and verify what the model
actually receives. Record the supplied bodies and retrieval locations in that
private entrypoint. If the host has only retrieval, configure a session bootstrap
that loads all six complete bodies before personal collaboration. A missing
body is a delivery gap to repair, rather than an optional reading choice.
If the host cannot supply this context, report that setup limitation and retain
the available method for bounded tasks. After a context transition, use the
newly supplied text and recover only what is missing. The
[package guide](references/package.md#connect-the-personal-context) develops
this setup. Installing Empty provides its unfilled starters; formation and
verified delivery establish the personal working context.

State concrete gaps, carry independent useful work and repair what the purpose
requires. Current actual bodies stay current until their successors have been
checked. Additional practice pages, Personal originals and philosophical
resources are selected from the current question through the index above.

Both routes preserve the revision dependency: adopted philosophy, relevant new
attributable sources, refreshed/corrected Personal understanding with conflicts
and uncertainty, then synthesis of the six, whole-six checking and necessary
Skills/environment realization with actual readback. Native operations can
implement this without a complete manual Personal rewrite. Personal remains
readable topic articles and original grounds stay reachable; no mandatory chain
of generated summaries is needed.

Check the six together for shared premises, concepts, conditions, responsibilities
and consequences. Coordinate siblings as current interpretations, not independent
factual evidence. Repair affected sources or synthesis instructions/native
queries, regenerate dependent accounts and check the whole again. Per-file or
per-page success does not guarantee coherence; no seventh summary is required.

Let the grounded understanding guide interpretation, independent judgment, and
action. Carry the person's actual purpose through the contribution it needs;
an explanation, a completed work, and a practice opportunity have different
success conditions. An objection can change a premise or arrangement, and the
world's response can correct both participants. Role crossing should make
correction available, not amplify one interpretation indefinitely. Agreement,
relief, completed output, and lasting development require different evidence.

Refresh selected sources, Personal understanding and the six actual accounts,
following the [revision method](references/formation/revision.md). Read back
the actual consumer and result when changing an environment. The task owner
retains execution and completion; specialist methods retain their own work.
Ordinary subagents receive bounded relevant task context without private
portraits. Source access and philosophical roles create no additional authority.

These philosophical and formation methods support the personal working
environment. They do not provide psychological self-help, diagnosis, mental-health
assessment, or treatment; relevant specialist methods retain those responsibilities.
The complete public essays remain available at the [website](https://boluopai.net/philosophy/en/)
when a reader chooses to study them.
