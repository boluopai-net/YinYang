## Begin with Empty

Once a version is formed, [revise its connected whole](../formation/revision.md#revise-the-collection-as-a-connected-whole):
philosophical arguments, Core views and their important tables,
working accounts, guides, and reading entrances develop together. The model
traces these dependencies from the person's request and carries the resulting
version into use.

The public Skill is **Empty**. The author's philosophical essays and diagrams remain available as reading material; your personal version develops its own adopted understanding.

**Empty provides the recursive self-improvement method.** Form personal content from your own purposes, experience, material, and judgment, then revise it through actual work.

The six accounts give this method a working form: direction, actual participants,
their cooperation, its realization, and the way it changes itself. Read them
together to understand an activity, explore a candidate change, compare its
effects, and carry the selected revision into use. The
[RSI essay](literary-recursive-improvement.md) develops the argument; the
[six writing guides](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
explain how to form the accounts from your own material.

[**Empty method source**](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/tree/main/skills/yinyang-empty) is the entrance for your own practice.

## Choose assistance for the same method

### Direct model writing

Give the language model you currently use your own philosophical understanding, Personal material, and existing accounts.
Use the [six formation guides](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
to condense, organize, and revise them directly. As model capabilities develop, let newer models reinterpret
the material and improve its condensation. Keep the six files in private `references/situated/`,
with versions in private Git/GitHub.

### Optional memory and synthesis assistance

Add a community project for memory and working-account formation to your Empty-based personal version.
This project uses [Hindsight](../hindsight.md): it retains sources, integrates observations, and forms
maintained accounts from questions. The model carries configuration, checking, and revision,
returning adopted results to the six accounts.
The [Hindsight configuration guide](../hindsight.md#configure-the-selected-native-implementation)
is the operational entrance.

Choose assistance by the actual retrieval, integration and maintenance work it
improves. Both implementations retain the same six questions, attributable
grounds, whole-account checking and return from use. Tool choice can change
as the material, model and maintenance costs change.

## Where the six accounts live and how to use them

Orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision live in private
`references/situated/`; the [file and update contract](skill-package.md#common-and-situated-content)
lists their filenames. Personal material, readable topic articles, and the source index live in
`references/personal/`. A native implementation can also bind the six complete bodies through a private index.

For formation and revision, the model reads adopted philosophy and relevant fresh sources, corrects Personal
understanding, synthesizes and reads the six accounts together, then realizes selected changes in the working
environment and revises from use. [Forming and revising working models](../design-and-revision.md)
develops the writing method; [everyday collaboration](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/blob/main/skills/yinyang-empty/SKILL.md) explains reading and using the current accounts.

## Begin with the material you already have

Existing philosophy, personal experience, preferences, works, or a working environment can provide the starting point.
The model helps form missing connections; actual work can also change the philosophy and collaboration.
For further writing, [An example and a commons](example-and-commons.md) opens a path to personal essays and different works.
